Everyone's a member here.

A fresh haircut does more than tidy you up. It changes how you walk into a job interview, a first day of school, a wedding, or an ordinary Monday. When you look sharp you feel it, and the people around you notice.

That's why so many of our clients have sat in the same chair for years. Jewel City Barbers Club is built on regulars: families who bring three generations in on the same Saturday, kids who grew up getting their cuts here, and neighbors who wouldn't trust anyone else with their fade.

Founded by Chris Craig and home to four barbers, we've earned our reputation as Glendale's premier barbershop one cut at a time, and we work to keep it every time someone sits down.
